# Executive Summary

**RFP #MC-2026-0417 — Inventory Dashboard Modernization**
**Submitted by:** Accenture
**Date:** April 28, 2026

---

Meridian Components has a dashboard that mostly works — and a team that can't fully rely on it. The Reports module has known defects that haven't been resolved. There is no automated test coverage, which has caused IT to block further changes. Tokyo warehouse staff work in English-only views. And the operations team is waiting on a Restocking capability that was never delivered.

The previous vendor left Meridian with unfinished work and thin documentation. We understand why confidence in outside vendors is low. Restoring that confidence is as much a part of this engagement as the technical deliverables.

Our approach is straightforward: we start with what's broken (Reports remediation and architecture documentation), establish the foundation that makes change safe (automated browser tests), and then build what's missing (Restocking recommendations). UI improvements and full i18n — particularly for the Tokyo team — are scoped as follow-on work within the same engagement.

We will not deliver a system and disappear. Every deliverable ships with documentation, test coverage, and a clear handoff to Meridian IT.

**What we're committing to:**

- Full audit and remediation of the Reports module, including all defects identified during discovery
- A new Restocking view that gives operations staff actionable purchase recommendations within a defined budget ceiling
- End-to-end browser test coverage across critical flows, sufficient for IT sign-off
- A current-state architecture overview written for Meridian IT, not for us
- i18n improvements prioritized for Tokyo warehouse staff
- A clean, professional UI refresh that doesn't require retraining your team

Meridian's operations team deserves a dashboard they can trust. That's what we're here to deliver.
