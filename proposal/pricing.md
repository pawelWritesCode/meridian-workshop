# Pricing

## Optional: Dark Mode (D3)

Dark mode (D3) is scoped as a fixed-fee add-on of **$4,000** if elected at contract signature. If added after work begins, pricing may change.

---

## Payment Terms

We propose milestone-based payments tied to the phase structure in our timeline:

- 25% at contract signature
- 25% at Phase 1 completion (architecture docs delivered, Reports audit complete)
- 25% at Phase 2 completion (Reports remediation and initial test coverage approved by IT)
- 25% at Phase 3 completion (all remaining deliverables accepted)

---

## Assumptions

- Scope is as described in our Technical Approach. Changes to scope requested after Phase 1 will be handled via a written change order.
- Client will provide timely access to the codebase and a designated point of contact for feedback at each milestone.
- Pricing assumes no third-party integrations (e.g., live supplier pricing feeds) beyond what is described in the Technical Approach.
- Travel is not included; all work is performed remotely.

---

## Relevant Experience

We have delivered comparable engagements for operations-facing web applications — modernizing legacy vendor codebases, establishing test coverage where none existed, and building inventory and procurement tools for mid-market clients. References are available on request.

We understand that Meridian's previous vendor experience has made timeline and delivery confidence more important than lowest price. We price to reflect the actual work, and we stand behind our milestones.
